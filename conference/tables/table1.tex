\footnotesize
All trained priors (GMM and diffusion): $N_{\rm train}=1.6\times10^5$ channels; BLER after 16 receiver iterations; 2560 blocks per SNR; SNR grid $-3{:}3{:}15$\,dB ($32\times4$: $-12{:}3{:}6$\,dB). Sparse specular $8\times4$ (all seeds): last-epoch EMA diffusion weights (best-validation weights not saved); other diffusion checkpoints: best-validation weights.
$^{\rm a}$Paired two-sided sign test on block failures at the three registered decision SNRs (grid points with the baseline's BLER in $[0.005,0.9]$, nearest 0.1 on a log scale). $\surd$: proposed fails less, $p<0.05$ at $\geq2$ of the 3 points; n.s.: neither side at $\geq2$ points; insuff.: fewer than 3 decision SNRs, or fewer than 2 of them with $\geq6$ discordant blocks. n.s.\ and insuff.\ mean ``not decided'', not ``no difference''. No multiplicity correction; labels are counted, not claimed individually.
$^{\rm b}$SNR at BLER 0.1 of the baseline (GMM prior in (B), (D)) minus that of the proposed method ($>0$: proposed better), 90\% paired-bootstrap CI; a report field, not the registered test. $\geq$: the baseline never reaches 0.1 on the grid. --: not measured (decision SNRs only).
$^{\rm c}$Discordant blocks summed over the decision SNRs: only the baseline failed\,:\,only the proposed method failed.
$^{\rm d}$Static labels vs.\ the 13 non-sparse baselines: post-hoc computation with a fixed rule on existing runs, after the curves had been seen (not pre-registered); the comparisons with the GMM prior, the Gaussian prior, both annealed-Langevin estimators and the pilot-only proposed prior were also pre-registered individually. Labels vs.\ the 3 sparse baselines: pre-registered; they use an on-grid oversampled 2-D DFT dictionary with oversampling factor 16 at the edge of the tuning grid (SBL at UMi: 8, inside), SBL (in loop) reuses the per-SNR iteration count tuned on pilot-only development NMSE, and OMP equals pilot LS at the decision SNRs of UMi and mixed (and at one of clustered SV).
$^{\rm e}$Fraction of the baseline-to-perfect-CSI gap closed, $(F_X-F_{\rm prop})/(F_X-F_{\rm PCSI})$, $F$ = block failures (of 2560) at $-3$\,dB; $X$ = the baseline in (A) (report-only), the GMM prior in (B) and (D); $^{\dagger}$$16\times4$ and $32\times4$ rows: failures summed over the three decision SNRs (a different statistic from the $-3$\,dB value). $R$ is relative to $X$ (it also rises when $X$ worsens). Perfect CSI: the same EP detector and BCJR loop given the true channel; a reference, not a bound.
$^{\rm f}$Priors trained on static (0$^\circ$) channels, applied without retraining to 6 settings (sparse specular $8\times4$ and $16\times4$; CN gains, clustered SV, UMi, mixed at $8\times4$) $\times$ \{in-block Doppler $\nu=0.005$, $0.01$; receive-array rotation $15^\circ$, $30^\circ$\}; counts of the 24 conditions; none with ``proposed fails more''. Sparse baselines were not run in these conditions.
$^{\rm g}$Diffusion checkpoint(s) not covered by the sample-quality gate (the sparse-specular $8\times4$ recipe passed it on a sibling testbed; in the non-stationary column and (C): all conditions except sparse specular $8\times4$): measurement labels, not method verdicts.
$^{\rm h}$Report-only adjunct point.
$^{\rm i}$GMM prior: $K$ chosen by validation likelihood; at the grid edge for sparse specular $8\times4$ ($K=1024$) and CN gains, UMi, mixed ($K=4096$); clustered SV $K=256$, inside the grid; (D) UMi $16\times4$, $32\times4$: $K=4096$ (grid edge), sparse specular $32\times4$: $K=2048$ (inside). EM stopped by the tolerance rule for the $K=4096$ fits and for sparse specular $32\times4$. The registered protocol, not an optimal GMM.
$^{\rm j}$Seed 1 is the reported model; seeds were not chosen after BLER. Label: seeds (of 3) with $\surd$ vs.\ the GMM prior.
$^{\rm k}$Seed 3 diverged in training and used the registered fallback (gradient clipping 1.0); under the earlier rule without this fallback: 2/3 and 1 not decided (seed 3 counted as a training failure; report-only).
$^{\rm l}$Fixed before the seed runs (only two decision SNRs on the grid); report-only.
$^{\rm m}$I.e., the GMM label is $\surd$ (19 of 24 conditions eligible), each of the other 12 labels is $\surd$, and the lower 90\% CI bound of $R$ vs.\ the best baseline (fewest failures at $-3$\,dB) is above 0.
$^{\rm n}$Pilot-only estimator using the proposed method's diffusion weights; not a reproduction of the original estimator.
$^{\rm o}$Read after the first receiver pass (registered rule for this baseline).
