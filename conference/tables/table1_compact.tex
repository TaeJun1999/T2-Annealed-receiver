Sparse: on-grid 2-D DFT dictionary, oversampling 16 at the tuning-grid edge; SBL in loop keeps pilot-tuned iteration counts; OMP = pilot LS at the decision SNRs of UMi, mixed (one of clustered SV).
$^{\rm e}$Static/0$^\circ$-trained priors, no retraining; \{sparse specular $8\times4$, $16\times4$; CN gains; clustered SV; UMi; mixed\} $\times$ \{Doppler $\nu=0.005$, $0.01$; rotation $15^\circ$, $30^\circ$\}. (C) row 4: lower 90\% CI bound of $R$ vs.\ the best baseline (fewest failures at $-3$\,dB) above 0; 19 conditions eligible (GMM label $\surd$).
$^{\rm f}$Fraction of the GMM-to-perfect-CSI gap closed, block failures at $-3$\,dB; relative to GMM. Perfect CSI: same EP detector and BCJR loop given the true channel; a reference, not a bound.
$^{\rm g}$Not covered by the sample-quality gate (unlike the sparse-specular $8\times4$ recipe): measurement labels, not method verdicts. $^{\rm h}$Report-only adjunct point.
$^{\rm i}$Of 3 diffusion seeds (seed 1 = reported). Sparse specular $32\times4$: seed 3 from the registered divergence fallback (gradient clipping); without it 2/3, 1 insuff.\ (report-only). Clustered SV: insuff.\ at all 3, fixed before the runs (two decision SNRs).
$^{\rm j}$GMM prior: $K$ by validation likelihood, at the grid edge ($K=1024$ sparse specular; 4096 CN gains, UMi incl.\ $16\times4$, $32\times4$, mixed); inside: clustered SV 256, sparse specular $32\times4$ 2048.
