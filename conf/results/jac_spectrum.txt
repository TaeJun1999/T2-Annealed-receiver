READING

1.  ON D1 THE LEARNED JACOBIAN IS RIGHT.  Its spectrum matches the exact one to three significant
    figures at every grid point (0.979/0.980, 0.866/0.867, 0.470/0.471, 0.118/0.117; lmin 0.94/0.92,
    0.68/0.62, 0.22/0.21, 0.031/0.039).  n_neg = 0 everywhere.  So the estimation procedure -- the
    architecture, the training objective, the Tweedie/Wirtinger conversion, the autodiff path -- is
    NOT the problem.  Given a smooth full-support target it recovers the second-order structure.
    This is the measurement behind the D1 BLER result: V0 == V1 (the PSD projection never fires) and
    the learned arm ties the exact-score oracle and the exact-EP bound.

2.  ON D2 IT IS NOT, AND MORE DATA MAKES IT WORSE.  n_neg goes 8.0 -> 10.3 (k=0) and 10.6 -> 17.0
    (k=6) when the budget is raised 16x; the 25th percentile falls to -0.003.  The learned spectrum
    crowds, and crosses, the PSD boundary.

3.  THE FITTED GMM IS NOT A REFERENCE HERE.  Its 25th percentile is 0.948 at k=0 where the learned
    model's is 0.028.  A mixture with full-rank component covariances cannot represent a density
    supported near a low-dimensional set, so its Jacobian stays near the identity and is trivially
    PSD.  Its PSD-ness is therefore NOT evidence that the learned model is wrong: the two models
    disagree about the local geometry, and on D2 we have no closed form to adjudicate.
    What we do have: two ground-truth-free diagnostics (GB' denoising, Hyvarinen score matching)
    both put the learned model CLOSER to the truth than the fitted GMM on D2.  That makes it
    plausible -- NOT proven -- that the near-zero spectrum is fidelity rather than noise.

4.  THE sigma DEPENDENCE EXPLAINS THE SNR SHAPE that the divergence diagnosis left open.
    On D2 the damage peaks at sigma ~ 0.09-0.26 (n_neg 14-17) and essentially VANISHES at
    sigma = 0.71 (n_neg 0.7, n<0.1 1.6).  The receiver's nu shrinks as SNR rises, so the operating
    point walks INTO the bad band as SNR increases -- which is the observed BLER shape
    (worst at 0..+3 dB, better at -3 dB where nu is large).  The settling experiment
    (code/diag_psd_cf.py, all seven SNRs) tests this prediction directly.

5.  CAVEAT on V1's identity claim.  selftest_M6 asserted the PSD projection is the identity on the
    exact prior because its lmin sits 780x above LAM_MIN=1e-6.  Measured over the full sigma grid
    that margin is smaller: the fitted GMM's lmin reaches 7.6e-5 at k=12, i.e. 76x.  The identity
    still holds, with two orders of margin rather than three.  Recorded so the claim is not quoted
    at the stronger figure.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH
  - It does not prove D2's true Jacobian is near-singular.  The manifold argument (D2's support is a
    union of 3L-dimensional sets, L~U{3..8}, i.e. 9..24 dimensions in R^64) is a PREDICTION from the
    generative model, and the measured n_neg of 8-17 is far below the 20-27 that argument suggested.
    The argument is consistent with the direction of the effect and wrong about its size.
  - It does not identify which of the 64 directions are tangent and which are normal; that would
    need the true tangent space, which the D2 generator can supply but this measurement did not use.
