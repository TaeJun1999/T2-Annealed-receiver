INTERPRETATION -- EXPLORATORY, NOT A PRE-REGISTERED TEST
====================================================================================================
Status of this file.  The pre-registered claim is the decision-point analysis in tables_D2.txt and
NOTHING here replaces it.  That analysis stands exactly as recorded: R2-ours-G -> M-ours-bstar is
significant at C2 (Tp=4, pooled 374:126, p=1.4e-29) and C4 (Tp=4, 47:5, p=1.3e-09), not significant
at C1 (Tp=2, p=0.69) and C5 (Tp=3, p=0.12), and REVERSED at C3 (Tp=2, first arm better, p=2e-06).
The read below is a secondary, exploratory look at the same frozen raw files with the SNR axis held
fixed instead of collapsed onto each cell's own three decision points.  It has no multiplicity
correction across the 7 SNRs and must not be quoted as a test.

What the matched-SNR read shows.
1.  On the 8x4 array the pooled discordant ratio rises monotonically with the pilot budget:
        Tp=2  1256:1157 = 1.09      Tp=3  442:359 = 1.23      Tp=4  392:131 = 2.99
2.  Within every cell the gain is concentrated at the LOW-SNR end and disappears, or turns negative,
    at the high end:
        C2 (Tp=4): -3 dB 274:79 (3.5x)  ->  +9..+15 dB nothing
        C5 (Tp=3): -3 dB  88:47         ->  +15 dB 36:64, i.e. the prior HURTS (p=6.6e-03)
        C3 (Tp=2): +9 dB  77:139 and +15 dB 88:132, both negative
3.  C3's reversal is therefore NOT a property of the 4x4 array.  C3's three decision points are all
    at the high-SNR end (+9/+12/+15), which is exactly where C5 also goes negative on the 8x4 array.
    The one place the two 4x4 cells overlap in SNR is +9 dB, same array, same T, only Tp differing:
        Tp=2  77:139  (p=3.0e-05, prior hurts)        Tp=4  7:1  (p=7.0e-02, prior helps)
    The sign flips with Tp at fixed SNR and fixed geometry.

A single reading that covers all five cells.  The structured prior helps when the channel estimate is
poor enough for prior information to matter, and hurts once the estimate is already good, because the
mixture then pulls the estimate toward component means it no longer needs.  Raising the pilot budget
widens the SNR range over which the first regime holds.  This is consistent with the mixture-EP
picture: with few pilots the component posterior is itself mis-assigned, and a confidently wrong
component is worse than no structure at all.

What this does NOT license.  It does not turn C1/C5 into positive results -- their pre-registered
tests are null and stay null.  It does not establish a threshold in Tp; three budgets on one array
and two on another cannot locate one.  A confirmatory test of the SNR-regime statement would need
its own pre-registration with the decision points chosen for that question, which was not done here.
