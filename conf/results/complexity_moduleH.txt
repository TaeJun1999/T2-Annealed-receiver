WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT SAY
  - It is INFERENCE cost only.  Training / fitting cost is separate and is already a column of
    TABLE A in every results table (GMM EM wall-clock); the score model's training wall-clock is
    in LADDER_C.md.  Neither is included here.
  - It is ONE implementation on ONE machine: numpy/BLAS for the GMM, torch.func.jacrev for the
    network, both float64 single-thread.  A different Jacobian implementation (forward-mode,
    batched, or an analytic head) would change the learned arm's number and not the GMM's.
  - The GMM cost scales with K; the learned cost does not.  At b* = K above, that is the
    comparison the BLER tables actually used.
  - If the learned arm is the more expensive one, then the equal-budget claim in this work is
    'same training data', NOT 'same inference complexity', and must be written that way.
