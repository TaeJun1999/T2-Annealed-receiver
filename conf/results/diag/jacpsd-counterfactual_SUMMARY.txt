3. TWO CORRECTIONS TO THE CLAIMANT'S SUPPORTING ARGUMENT (the verdict survives both)
================================================================================================
[C1] "non-symmetric, indefinite" -- only INDEFINITENESS is causal.  ScorePrior._eval Hermitian-
     symmetrises J before the receiver sees it, and every intervention above operates on the already-
     symmetrised Herm(J); asymmetry (herm_res 0.195) is a symptom of the same non-conservative score
     field, not a link in the causal chain.  Likewise the claimant's split table attributes the blow-up
     to "the NEGATIVE eigenvalue" while their two groups differ in BOTH sign and conditioning
     (|lmin| 2.9e-3 for the diffusion vs 9.9e-2 for the GMM).  abs1e-2 / tiny4 separate the two and
     show the sign is the whole story; the magnitude only sets the GAIN (damage ~ 1/|eig|), which is
     why injecting the sign at the GMM's own eigenvalue magnitude (flip4, |eig| 33x larger) produces
     only a mild 0.109 -> 0.141 and does not reproduce the collapse.
[C2] the receiver-side "mean relative belief-mean shift" (their 4.3e3 - 1.1e5 table) is a MARKER of
     the defect, not a dose.  RouteAClip records shift from the PRE-recompute eta regardless of clip
     mode (Demo/t2_gmm.py:120 precedes line 121), so dscore|mean records shift 1.146e+03 at 0 dB --
     the same order as the pre-registered arm -- while achieving BLER 0.047.  Their "shift peaks at
     6 dB, NMSE peaks at 6 dB" correspondence is therefore weaker evidence than they presented, and
     their own table already breaks it (shift is MINIMUM at 12 dB, 1.61e3, where BLER is still 0.523).
